Start with the big idea.

These three facts connect everything on your worksheets.

01 / CELLS

Life’s building blocks

All living organisms are made of cells. A cell is the smallest unit that can carry out life processes. Most cells need a microscope to be seen.

02 / ORGANELLES

Different jobs, one team

Organelles are specialized structures inside cells. Their jobs work together to keep the cell—and the organism—alive.

03 / EUKARYOTIC

A nucleus inside

Plant and animal cells are eukaryotic: they have a nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles. Both carry out many of the same basic life processes.

Four mix-ups to avoid.

Nucleus ≠ nucleolus

The nucleus stores DNA. The nucleolus is inside it and helps assemble ribosome parts.

Cell wall ≠ cell membrane

The rigid wall supports a plant cell. The flexible membrane controls what enters and leaves. A plant cell has both.

Chloroplasts ≠ mitochondria

Chloroplasts use light to make glucose. Mitochondria release energy from nutrients to produce ATP. Plant cells need mitochondria too.

Rough ER ≠ smooth ER

Rough ER has ribosomes and helps make and move proteins. Smooth ER has no attached ribosomes and helps make lipids.
