NARRATION CREDITS

This study guide contains locally generated synthetic narration in American English.
Voice preset: af_heart.
Model: Kokoro-82M v1.0 by hexgrad; the model weights are Apache-2.0.
Upstream model card: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
Local ONNX runtime implementation: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
Model files: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/tag/model-files-v1.0

The upstream card credits Koniwa (CC BY 3.0) and SIWIS (CC BY 4.0)
as training datasets. These files are newly synthesized narration, not recordings
copied from either dataset. The app does not ship model weights or voice packs.

File key: o = original worksheet, p = practice; q = question and choices,
w = worked solution and final answer. Correct choices are never identified
in the prompt tracks.
